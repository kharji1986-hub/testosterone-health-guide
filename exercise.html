Best Exercises for Testosterone Health

1. Strength Training

Resistance training helps build muscle, improve strength, and support overall health.

2. Squats

Squats strengthen your legs and engage several major muscle groups.

3. Cardiovascular Exercise

Walking, cycling, and running support cardiovascular health and fitness.

4. Recovery and Rest

Allow your muscles time to recover between demanding workouts.

Benefits of Regular Exercise

  • Improves muscle strength
  • Supports cardiovascular health
  • Helps maintain a healthy weight
  • Improves sleep quality
  • Reduces stress
