Common Supplements

Vitamin D

Important for overall health. Correcting a deficiency may support normal hormone function.

Zinc

Essential for normal testosterone production, especially when zinc intake is inadequate.

Magnesium

Supports normal muscle and nerve function.

Ashwagandha

Some small studies suggest possible benefits, but evidence remains limited.

Important Note

Supplements are not a substitute for medical treatment. Consult a healthcare professional before taking them.
