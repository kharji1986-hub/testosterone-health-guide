Exercises That Support Testosterone Health

Strength Training

Resistance training supports muscle strength, physical fitness, and overall health. Testosterone may rise temporarily after exercise, but this does not necessarily mean a lasting increase in resting testosterone levels.

Recommended Exercises

  • Squats
  • Deadlifts
  • Bench Press
  • Pull-ups
