Exercises That Support Testosterone Health

Strength Training

Resistance training supports muscle strength, physical fitness, and overall health. Testosterone may rise temporarily after exercise, but this does not necessarily mean a lasting increase in resting testosterone levels.

Recommended Exercises

  • Squats
  • Deadlifts
  • Bench Press
  • Pull-ups

Foods and Nutrition

Eggs

Eggs provide protein and important nutrients that support overall health.

Oysters and Seafood

Oysters are rich in zinc, a mineral needed for normal testosterone production. Zinc supplementation mainly helps when a zinc deficiency is present.

Fatty Fish

Salmon and sardines provide omega-3 fatty acids and vitamin D, supporting general health. They are not proven testosterone boosters.

Nuts and Olive Oil

These foods provide healthy fats that help maintain a balanced diet.
